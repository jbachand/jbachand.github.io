Reality check. The inaudible band on normal phone/laptop hardware is only ~18–19.8 kHz, so throughput is tens of bytes/sec over a few meters — great for text, links, codes, and contact cards; not for big files. "Inaudible" holds for most adults, but teens, kids, and pets can often hear it. If ultrasonic won't decode on your hardware, switch to Audible to confirm the protocol works, then tune from there.
